## Checklist answers (map Section 4.4)

- Q17 (fee ceiling). None. `bump` compounds 10% per resubmission over the previous submission on both fields (`tx/fees.rs:39-56`, `tx/types.rs:119-120`), the cap applies to fresh estimates only (`tx/mod.rs:396-409`, `tx/fees.rs:38`), and no absolute limit exists (`tx/config.rs:15-25`). Executed: 9.8x the first tip after 48 blocks, 97x after 96, 9,412x after 192 (`logs/R3-fee-config-probe.txt`). What stops it: only the node's balance check (the last accepted version reserves about the whole balance) or inclusion. Cost: the inflated tip on the gas actually used, up to most of the signer balance for one action. Filed as `F3-CORE-060` (Low, conditional). In Batched mode there are no own nonce gaps (one in flight), so the nonce-gap trigger is Direct-mode only and transient in every path I traced.
- Q18 (`priority_fee_cap_percentage` edge values). NaN and negatives give a zero tip (`f64::max` returns the non-NaN operand), infinity and values of 100 or more are no-ops, all executed in probe section A. A zero tip never grows under `bump_fee`. Filed with the other unchecked settings as `F3-CORE-061` (Informational).
- Q19 (executed marks from `eth_getProof` at `latest`). Stale-low answer (issue #961's case): the row stays in flight, is resubmitted, gets "nonce too low", logged as a plain failure (`tx/mod.rs:354-360`), and is marked on the next correct read; allocation is unaffected because `MAX(nonce)+1` over existing rows wins (`tx/storage.rs:190-195`); pruning is unaffected (only executed rows are pruned, `:329-344`). Cost: in Batched mode the next batch waits a block. High answer from a sibling block: observation O3. Startup and reorg unmarking are correct (`tx/mod.rs:188-200`), and `executed_at` is the observing block, never earlier than inclusion, so unmarking is conservative and pruning at `executed_at <= safe` never drops a reorg-able row.
- Q20 (underpriced matching). Matches geth-family "replacement transaction ... underpriced" and Ankr's "INTERNAL_ERROR: could not replace existing tx" (ethers.js heuristics per `80f747c`). A miss takes the plain-failure path: the floor and `submitted_at` stay, so the row is resubmitted every block at `max(fresh, floor * 1.1)`, the same rejected price until the estimate moves. Observation O2 (client wording unverified offline).
- Q21 (expiry only before allocation). Confirmed: `expires_at` is checked only for `nonce IS NULL` rows (`tx/storage.rs:172-178, 212-220`); allocated rows are resubmitted until executed. Late landing of the listed actions reverts or no-ops in the contracts (commit and reveal windows, coordinator phases), so the cost is gas, not safety. Observation O4.
- Q22 (authorization-gap cancellation in Batched mode). Sent as a plain EIP-1559 transaction, not bundled: `stale_submissions` returns the stored `Transaction::default()` request (`tx/storage.rs:389-402`) and `submit_transaction` signs it as is (`tx/mod.rs:313-325`); with no delegate at the next nonce it carries no authorization (`tx/signer.rs:65-80`). It is valid while delegated: a delegated EOA may originate transactions, the target is `0x0`, 21,000 gas covers intrinsic cost with empty calldata (EIP-7623 floor is also 21,000). It counts as the one in-flight transaction (`tx/storage.rs:131-139`).
- Q23 (batch gas formula). No concrete shortfall found for Aegis actions (derivation, not executed). The only reservation that is not consumed by a call is `ceil(g/63)`, so headroom at call i is at least `5,000 - pre_i - base_i` plus the later calls' reservations plus `ceil(g_j/63) + 5,000 - o_j` for every earlier call j (`tx/bundle.rs:49-74, 195-209` against `Safenet7702Executor.sol:96-111`). The first call needs `pre + base` of at most 5,000 (cold access 2,600 plus decode and copy, roughly 3,000). An out-of-gas callee returns no data, so its post-call cost is the 1,518-gas `CallFailed` log plus copies; a reverting callee with data returns its unused gas. Calldata: the EIP-7623 floor (10 gas per token, up to 40 per nonzero byte) stays below the limit while `sum(gas) > 24 * calldata bytes`, true for every validator and sentinel action (gas 55,000 to 400,000, calldata under about 1 KB; the validator callback context carries hashes only, `state/sign.rs:785-799`). Value calls: none of the encoders sets value (`service/action.rs`, sentinel `service.rs:864-937`). L5 (no worst-case test) remains as settled.
- Q24 (bundler set versus `UPDATE`). Same set: one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction (`tx/storage.rs:165`), identical filter and block binding (`:173-177`, `:213-218`), rows taken as a prefix in `ORDER BY id`, `id <= last` selects exactly that prefix, a deserialization failure aborts the transaction, and new rowids are always above existing ones.

## Invariants (map Section 3.1)

- INV-C7 holds: allocation picks `MAX(account nonce, MAX(allocated)+1)` inside `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` (`tx/storage.rs:165, 190-195`); the delegate row takes `nonce + 1` in the same transaction (`:205-211`) and the authorization is signed for `tx.nonce + 1` (`tx/signer.rs:82-86`). Primary-key collisions are impossible since both nonces exceed the current maximum. The table is empty after pruning, in which case allocation relies on the account nonce alone; a by-number read cannot return a nonce below a row executed at or below the safe block without a reorg deeper than `max_reorg_depth` (A5).
- INV-C8 holds, with the nuance above: unmark on startup above the safe block and on any decrease of `latest` (`tx/mod.rs:188-200`); every reorg reaches the queue as a decrease, because the block watcher lowers `pending` on both uncle paths (`index/blocks.rs:476-489`, `:571-590`) and the driver calls `update_block_status` on every update (`driver.rs:242-249`).
- INV-C9 holds: both fields rise to at least 110% of the last accepted or underpriced submission (`tx/fees.rs:53-56`, `tx/mod.rs:333-350`); a zero tip stays zero, which still satisfies replacement rules (F3-CORE-061).
- INV-C10 holds: one in flight in Batched mode (`tx/config.rs:65-74`), self-calls to `self.signer.address()` (`tx/mod.rs:257`, `tx/bundle.rs:170-177`), startup refuses an executor without code and a batch limit above half the latest block gas limit (`tx/mod.rs:111-136`), the zero executor is a config error (`tx/config.rs:118-123`), chain id 0 is never signed (`tx/signer.rs:59-63`). After a reorg two batches can be unexecuted at once (the delegating batch and its successor); the mempool then rejects the successor until the first lands, and it self-heals.

## Rejected hypotheses

1. Nonce reuse after pruning: rejected, `MAX(account nonce, ...)` with by-number reads (`tx/storage.rs:190-195`, `tx/mod.rs:372-380`); see INV-C7.
2. Authorization-gap cancellation triggered without external use (stale read, reorg, replacement without the authorization): rejected. Every version of the delegating transaction is rebuilt from the same row with the authorization joined from the delegate row (`tx/storage.rs:389-399`), and a self-sponsored authorization for `nonce + 1` is always valid after the transaction's own nonce increment, on any fork.
3. A third party replaying a broadcast authorization tuple to delegate or undelegate the account: rejected. The tuple is valid only at account nonce `N + 1`, which the queue's own transaction at `N` consumes first; included earlier it is skipped as invalid.
4. Batched-mode cancellation invalid while delegated: rejected (Q22).
5. Batch gas shortfall from memory growth: rejected. Memory is never freed in the executor loop, but for a 2,000,000-gas batch of sentinel calls total memory stays under 1,000 words (about 3,500 gas of expansion), against a 5,000-per-call budget.
6. EIP-7623 floor making a batch or a Direct transaction invalid: rejected for every Aegis action (Q23).
7. Attacker-sized calldata in validator actions pushing intrinsic gas over the fixed limits: rejected, the attestation callback carries `safeTxStructHash` and `oracleDataHash` only (`crates/validator/src/state/sign.rs:785-799`).
8. `bump` producing `maxPriorityFeePerGas > maxFeePerGas`: rejected, each field is bumped from a pair that already satisfies the order, and `max(a, b)` preserves it.
9. Overflow in `cap_priority_fee`: rejected, `base_fee + capped` never exceeds the original `max_fee_per_gas`; the `f64 as u128` cast saturates.
10. SQLite `SQLITE_BUSY` without a busy wait in the queue: rejected. The default journal mode is the rollback journal (sqlx sets none); the only read-then-write transaction (`next_transaction`) starts with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, and `enqueue` and `prune` start with a write.
11. Foreign-key violation in `prune`: rejected, transactions referencing the pruned nonce rows are deleted first with the same condition in the same transaction (`tx/storage.rs:329-344`).
12. Secrets in logs from the queue: rejected. `Signer`'s `Debug` prints the address (`tx/signer.rs:174-178`), error types carry no data (`:19-27`), logs carry nonce, block, hash and the RPC error (whose URL the provider strips).
13. Panics on untrusted input in the seven files: none. The only `expect` is on a literal regex (`tx/mod.rs:423`).
14. `stale_submissions` JSON assembly losing or misreading fields: rejected, the delegate is stored with `Address::to_string`, fees as hex quantities read by `alloy::serde::quantity::opt`, and a NULL join yields `authorization: null`.
15. Replacement rejected because the bump is exactly 10%: rejected for geth-family and Nethermind-style rules (`old + old/10` floor), since the queue adds `ceil(old/10)`.

## Observations (plausible, not proven)

- O1. Batched-mode throughput at the default `max_batch_gas` (2,000,000) is lower than Direct mode for 250,000-gas actions: about 7 sentinel actions per block (each reserves about 264,000 with `ceil(g/63)`, 5,000 and calldata) against 16 in Direct mode, and about 1.8 sentinel requests per block (approve, commit, reveal, finalize, claim; `crates/sentinel/src/service.rs:235-251, 436-447, 680-686`). The handbook pitches batching as improving throughput (`docs/validator-handbook.md:77`). A sustained flood of proposals (each costing the proposer the oracle fee) therefore saturates a batching sentinel sooner. Design and docs matter, for R7 and R8.
- O2. Underpriced wording: if a Gnosis client rejects an underpriced replacement with other wording (I recall Nethermind returning `ReplacementNotAllowed`, unverified offline), and the node already holds a higher-fee version than the recorded floor (a lost send response), the queue resends the same rejected price every block. It stalls only if that held version is also below the base fee; it then clears when the base fee falls back or the fresh tip estimate rises 10% over the held version. PoC needs a Nethermind or Erigon node; Anvil uses geth wording.
- O3. Execution marker from a sibling block: `eth_getProof` is read by number (`tx/mod.rs:372-380`), and issue #961 shows a by-number answer that disagreed with the canonical block. If an answer comes from a sibling that included nonce N while the canonical block did not, N is marked executed and never resubmitted (`tx/storage.rs:281-292, 400`). Recovery relies on N still being in mempools (orphaned-block transactions are re-added), so it is normally harmless; it hurts only if N then becomes unincludable, in which case Direct-mode nonces above it stall and compound fees (F3-CORE-060). A self-check (re-reading the nonce at the next block and unmarking rows at or above it) would close it.
- O4. Late actions after expiry: allocated rows ignore `expires_at` and land late; every late validator or sentinel action I checked reverts or no-ops, so the cost is gas per late call (Batched: each reverting call emits `CallFailed`).
- O5. Batched-mode latency: the driver reconciles the queue before the state machine handles the update (`driver.rs:244-261`), so under continuous load the batch submitted at block X carries actions from earlier blocks and block X's own actions wait for the next batch, about two blocks per action. Signing (6 blocks) and the Aegis commit and reveal windows (6 blocks each) absorb this, but a late engine check (up to 18.75 s) plus a busy queue leaves little room. For R7 (Q51).
- O6. geth-family mempools track pending authorizations per authority, so a third party holding any authorization the account ever signed (public once broadcast) could keep a sponsored SetCode transaction pending and limit the account to one in-flight transaction in those pools. No effect in Batched mode (already one), and Gnosis runs Nethermind and Erigon, whose rules I could not check offline.
- O7. The queue does not deduplicate content: actions replayed after a restart or reorg are queued again. Cost is gas for reverting duplicates; owned by R2 (Q16).
- O8. Operability: no metric for in-flight count, resubmission rounds, recorded fee level or signer balance; an unfunded or stuck signer shows only as repeated `submission failed` warnings and the per-method RPC failure counter.
- O9. Offline reorg and startup pruning: on the first status after a restart the queue prunes at the new safe block before it unmarks above it (`tx/mod.rs:183-200`). Rows marked executed on a branch that was reorged while the service was down, within the window at the time but now at or below the new safe block, are deleted without re-checking the account nonce. Harm needs those transactions not to be re-included on the new branch during the downtime and the replay not to re-emit the action; both are unusual (mempools re-add orphaned transactions, and the state machine replays from its oldest snapshot). Same logic at `fe9e84c`. Queue-side twin of R1's Q1.
