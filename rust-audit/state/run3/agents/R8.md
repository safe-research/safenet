### Q23 (second reader of `tx/bundle.rs`)

No concrete shortfall. The executor's check (`gasleft() * 63 / 64 >= call.gasLimit`, contracts/src/Safenet7702Executor.sol:104) is met by the `⌈g/63⌉` term; the 5,000 per call covers the `CALL` base cost (2,600 cold, 100 warm) and the loop; value calls add 34,000 (9,000 transfer plus 25,000 possible account creation). Each inner call receives the action's full gas limit, which in Direct mode already pays 21,000 intrinsic and the calldata, so every call is over-reserved by at least 21,000. Memory growth across a batch is bounded through the 16 gas per calldata byte term by `max_batch_gas`, and the slack left by earlier calls (at least `⌈g/63⌉` plus about 1,900 each) exceeds it for Safenet's action shapes (55,000 to 250,000 gas per sentinel action, small calldata). The authorization adds `PER_EMPTY_ACCOUNT_COST` 25,000 (bundle.rs:182-184). The missing worst-case test is settled item L5. Worked example, a sentinel `ApproveToken` (55,000) and `Commit` (250,000) batched together, each with 68 bytes of calldata: batch gas 356,147; with a worst-case intrinsic cost of 33,904 (21,000, 16 gas for every calldata byte, 2,600 for loading the delegation target) execution starts with 322,243; if the approve burns its whole limit plus 2,600 base and 500 loop overhead, 264,143 remain before the commit, which needs 256,569 (its limit times 64/63 plus 2,600), a margin of 7,574 (computed with a short script from the constants at bundle.rs:71-74 and :196-209).

## Observations (plausible, not proven)

- O-1 How often the undercount of F3-XC-001 happens on Aegis production depends on engine latency differences between sentinels; not measured. An end-to-end reproduction on Anvil with one engine delayed by more than one block (QA, Phase 3) would settle it.

A `cargo update` within 3.x could change them without a semver signal. Pinning `=3.0.0` makes updates deliberate. The lock pins 3.0.0 today.
