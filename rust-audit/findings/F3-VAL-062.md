## Claim

While a validator waits for its own `Resume::Setup` after every commitment of a key generation is in (`RolloverState::CollectingCommitments` with `secrets: None`), `handle_key_gen_secret_shared` drops every `KeyGenSecretShared` event for that group, because it only matches `CollectingShares`. A warp page applies `NewBlock` and all logs of up to 100 blocks in one `handle_update`, and effects only start after that update returns, so after a restart the re-emitted `Effect::KeyGenSetup` always resumes after every commitment and share in the same page. The dropped shares are never delivered again: the validator sits in `CollectingShares` with an incomplete `shared` set, never confirms, and loses that epoch's group; the other members wait for the confirm deadline (360 blocks) before restarting without it. The late-setup path also sets the share deadline from the commitment deadline (`deadline + key_gen_timeout`) instead of the last commitment's block, so even without a dropped share the restarted validator times out the share round about 119 blocks after its peers (when the last commitment lands one block after the start).

## Trigger

Aegis parameters: Gnosis 5 s blocks, `max_reorg_depth` 5, `blocks_per_epoch` 1440, `key_gen_timeout` 120, `block_page_size` 100, five validators with threshold 3, `min_participants(5)` = 4.

1. Epoch N's key generation starts at block `b0 = (N - 1) * 1440` on every validator. Validator B (honest) commits in `b0 + 1` with the other four; the five `KeyGenSecretShared` events land in `b0 + 3 .. b0 + 5`.
2. B is stopped at `b0 + 3` (a container restart or upgrade about 15 s after the epoch boundary). Its retained snapshots cover about `b0 - 2 .. b0 + 3`.
3. B comes back at `b0 + 10` (35 s later). `initialize` queues `Uncle { b0 - 1 }` and `Warp { b0 - 1 ..= b0 + 5 }`. That single page applies `NewBlock(b0)` (start_key_gen, `Effect::KeyGenSetup`), the five commitments of `b0 + 1` (row 5: wait for setup) and all five shares (row 6: dropped).
4. After the page the setup resumes: B enters `CollectingShares` with `shared` empty and share deadline `b0 + 240`. No share is delivered again, so B never confirms. At `b0 + 240` B excludes every member it saw no share from (row 8): with all five shares dropped nobody is left and B skips the epoch (`rollover_failure`); with one share dropped, as in row 10, B restarts into a four-member group that nobody else joins.
5. The other four reach `CollectingConfirmations` at `b0 + 5` and exclude B at the confirm deadline `b0 + 365`, then restart with four members and stage the epoch before `b0 + 1440`. B sits out epoch N (two hours), and the epoch's group is delayed by about 30 minutes.
6. If two validators restart in that window (for example a coordinated upgrade), the remaining three are below `min_participants(5)` = 4 and the epoch is skipped. With no share dropped (B back before the shares are reorg-safe) but one malicious member withholding its share, the peers restart at `b_last + 120` while B restarts at `b0 + 240` (row 7); with `b_last = b0 + 1` the peers' commitment deadline for the restarted group is `b0 + 241`, and B's commitment, queued at `b0 + 240`, lands in `b0 + 241` at the earliest, after `NewBlock(b0 + 241)` has already timed it out. B is excluded too and the epoch is skipped.

The window is roughly the first 25 s after each epoch boundary (every two hours), for a validator whose own commitment is already mined and that stays down until the first share is five blocks deep.

## Considered and rejected

- Live reorgs: after an `Uncle` the watcher emits one `New` block and one `Logs` update at a time, each needing an RPC round trip, while the setup effect is a local SQLite write, so the resume almost always lands between updates. Not claimed as a trigger.
- A validator that was down for the whole commitment round never committed, so its own late commitment closes the round for everyone and all validators derive the same share deadline; the drop needs the restarting validator's own commitment to be inside the warp range.
- Other resumes do not gate events: `CollectNonceCommitments` and `CollectSigningShares` record peer events whether or not `GenerateNonces` or `UseNonces` resumed (state/sign.rs:243-301, 399-412).
- Redelivery: no later path brings the dropped events back; the snapshot committed after the page records the state without them, and the watcher does not refetch logs below its cursor.
- Run 2 commit `fe9e84c`: warp pages applied no `NewBlock` (`git show fe9e84c:crates/core/src/state/mod.rs`, the `Update::Logs` arm only applies `Message::Event`), so a restart across the key generation start did not start it during the warp at all. The current mechanism comes with #1017 (`24eaf8d`, per-block `NewBlock` in warps); the loss of the epoch on such a restart is older, the dropped-share path is new.

## Remediation options

1. Keep the group's share, complaint, response and confirmation events while `CollectingCommitments` waits for its setup (for example a `pending` list in that state) and replay them when `Resume::Setup` finalizes the round; store the last commitment's block so both exits derive the share deadline from it. Contained in keygen.rs; needs care to keep the snapshot small.
2. In the core driver, end a warp page after any transition that spawns an effect, so resumes land before the next block's logs. Fixes every service at once; costs warp throughput when many effects are spawned.
3. Have `handle_key_gen_committed` treat "all committed, setup outstanding" as `CollectingShares` with the secrets still pending, verifying buffered shares once they arrive.

Tests to add: a transition test that feeds the commitments and a share before `Resume::Setup` (as in the E1 log) and expects the share counted and the share deadline equal to the live path's; an integration test that stops a validator right after its commitment is mined and restarts it once the shares are reorg-safe.

## QA second reproduction (QA3-2A, 2026-10-09)

Result: Reproduced.

Route: a different warp shape from the single-dropped-share case. The warp page that replays the key-generation start carries ALL FIVE peer KeyGenSecretShared events before the local Resume::Setup. Driven against the real code at 361aaf3 through Transition::apply_transition in warp order (NewBlock(1440); five KeyGenCommitted at 1441, own included; all five KeyGenSecretShared at 1443..1447; then Resume::Setup): every share is dropped (the handler matches only CollectingShares), so the round finalizes with shared={} and a share deadline of 1680 (derived from the commitment deadline 1560 + key_gen_timeout, not the last commitment block 1441 + key_gen_timeout = 1561, the drift of basis 7). At NewBlock(1680) the validator excludes the whole group (exclude_all_others of the empty set) and reaches EpochSkipped(2): the stronger single-validator outcome of trigger step 4. A live-order control (setup before the shares) keeps every share, reaches CollectingConfirmations, and shows the 1561 deadline, confirming both the drop and the drift are ordering-dependent.

Command and output: poc/F3-VAL-062/repro-2/ (qa2a_val062_all_shares_dropped.rs, RUN.md, baseline.out). `cargo test --offline -p validator --bins qa2a_val062 -- --nocapture`.

Bite check: remediation option 1/3 applied (buffer shares seen while the group waits for its setup, keyed by gid on State.pending_shares, and replay them after handle_key_gen_setup finalizes into CollectingShares). The warp test then reaches CollectingConfirmations (all five replayed), so the `shared == {}` assertion fails (with-fix.out). Remediation sound for the dropped-share effect; the deadline drift needs the separate "store the last commitment block" part of option 1.

Certainty: 90 (Reproduced plus Critic Confirmed; transition order and restart warp both executed on real code; the all-dropped route independently lands on EpochSkipped).
